About

A dedicated PDF editor.

PDFDR helps you select existing PDF text, replace or erase it, add images and annotations, manage pages and export a PDF.

Known limitations

Embedded fonts, scanned pages, rotated pages, complex backgrounds and unusual layouts can affect accuracy. Full paragraph reflow and OCR are not supported.
